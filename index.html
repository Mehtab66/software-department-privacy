Privacy policy

Software Department Scheduler · Last updated 8 October 2026

This policy describes the LinkedIn Scheduler app run by Software Department. The app drafts posts and publishes them to the LinkedIn account of the person who signs in.

Information from LinkedIn

When you choose Sign in with LinkedIn, LinkedIn sends us:

  • Your name, email address, and profile photo
  • Your LinkedIn member id
  • An access token, and a refresh token if LinkedIn provides one

The token is stored encrypted and is used only to publish posts you approve. LinkedIn does not give this app your headline or About section.

Information you enter

You can save a profile the app uses to personalize drafts:

  • Title, industry, about, target audience, tone, usual topics, and timezone
  • Post text, topics, and the date and time you choose for each post

How the information is used

  • To show your name and photo in the app
  • To generate draft posts from the profile and topic you provide
  • To publish a post to your LinkedIn account when you post it or when its scheduled time arrives
  • To keep a session so you stay signed in

Draft generation sends your saved profile and the topic to the model provider configured for the app. The default provider is xAI (Grok). The provider can be changed by the person who operates the app.

Publishing sends the post text to LinkedIn. LinkedIn’s own privacy policy applies to content that appears on LinkedIn.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information. We do not use it to post anything you have not saved or scheduled. We do not ask LinkedIn for your connections, messages, or private activity.

How long it is kept

Profile details and posts stay in the app database until you delete them, or until the operator of this app deletes the account. Signing out removes the session cookie from your browser. It does not delete saved drafts. LinkedIn access lasts about 60 days. Signing in again replaces the stored token.

Cookies

The app sets an httpOnly session cookie so the server can tell who is signed in, and a short-lived cookie during LinkedIn sign-in to prevent a forged login response.
